How to Attempt a Test

Follow these simple steps to securely attempt your online quiz!
  1. Click the Attempt button below to go to the test search page.
  2. Enter the test name or keyword to find your quiz.
  3. Select your test from the results and click Attempt.
  4. Fill in your details (such as name and email) if prompted.
  5. The test will start in fullscreen mode with anti-cheat enabled.
  6. Answer each question within the allotted time.
  7. Submit your answers when finished. Your results will be logged automatically.
